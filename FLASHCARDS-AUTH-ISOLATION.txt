FLASHCARDS AUTHENTICATION ISOLATION

When Flashcards is opened inside Classroom Tools, the iframe loads:
  flashcards.html?embedded=classroom

Embedded Flashcards uses its own named Firebase app with LOCAL authentication persistence.
This means:
- After a teacher signs in once, embedded Flashcards remembers that login on the same browser/device.
- Reopening Classroom Tools or returning to Flashcards does NOT require signing in again.
- The remembered embedded login remains until the teacher explicitly clicks Sign out (or clears browser site data).
- Signing in/out of embedded Flashcards does not sign the standalone Flashcards page in/out.
- The standalone Flashcards page keeps its own normal remembered Firebase login.
- Classroom Tools' This device / Google account setting remains independent.

No Firebase rules change is required for this update.
